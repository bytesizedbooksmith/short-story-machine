How It Works

You plan the story with the AI's help. The AI drafts it, checks it and hands it back. You only stop three times.

Setup (first time only)

  • 0
    SetupYou
    Your pen name, genre, point of view and tense, content limits, and anything you already love or hate in AI writing. About five minutes. It's saved as your style sheet and used for every story.

Planning

A conversation. The AI asks a few questions at a time and offers options. You can always say "you choose."

  • 1
    IdeaYou
    Your idea, genre, length, and what a reader should feel at the end.
  • 2
    PeopleYou
    Your main character: what they want, what's in the way and what they could lose. Plus anyone else the story needs.
  • 3
    ShapeYou
    The story from opening to ending, the main character's key choices, and a one-paragraph summary.
  • 4
    Scene MapYou
    The AI turns the shape into scene cards. You check the order and choose your opening from three options. See Good Hooks.
  • 5
    Plan Challenge
    A separate reviewer hunts for gaps: timing that doesn't work, people who show up before they're introduced, a hook that comes too late. The AI fixes what it can.
  • 6
    ✋ Approve The PlanYou
    You see the summary, the scene list and anything the challenger raised. Nothing is drafted until you say yes. You can also share a page of your own finished prose for the draft to sound like, or skip that.

Writing

The AI does the work. You read the opening, then wait for the draft.

  • 7
    Writing Plan
    The AI writes instructions for each scene, then checks that none of them contradict each other or your style sheet.
  • 8
    ✋ Opening SampleYou
    The AI drafts Scene 1, has it checked, then asks you to read it. The checks test the hook and the plan; only you can judge the voice. Your notes ("less description," "more casual") carry into every scene after, and the version you approve becomes the voice example for the rest. If it isn't working, you can edit it yourself or bring your own version.
  • 9
    Draft The Rest
    One scene at a time. Progress is saved after each one.
  • 10
    Three Audits
    Three reviewers read the whole draft separately: a simulated Reader (an AI reading like a reader: is it clear and gripping? It's not human feedback), Continuity (do the facts and timeline hold?) and Style (does it follow your style sheet and avoid AI habits?).
  • 11
    Revise
    The AI fixes what the audits found. Two rounds at most, so it doesn't rewrite your story into mush.
  • 12
    ✋ Final Check And HandoffYou
    One last check, then you get your draft and a short note: what was checked, what was fixed, and where to look first when you edit.

After Handoff

The draft is yours. Edit it on your own, or ask the AI for help in the same chat. After a bigger edit, the AI tells you which checks the new version hasn't had and offers a recheck for continuity and repetition. When you're ready for another story, type start.

Writing With A Different AI

Prefer another model's prose? Tell the Guide. For each scene, it prepares a pack you can copy into the other app, saves what comes back exactly as returned, and checks it like any other draft. Planning, approvals and checks all stay the same.

What's A Scene?

A scene is one continuous stretch of story: one time and place, one point of view. Someone wants something, runs into resistance, and something is different by the end. If nothing changes, it isn't a scene yet.

A new scene starts when time jumps, the place changes after a break, the point of view changes, or the scene's question is settled and a new one begins. Someone walking in, a brief memory, or a scene getting long doesn't start a new one on its own.

What Can Characters Know?

Your point-of-view character knows only what they've seen, been told (which may be wrong), can work out, or learned from their past, by that moment in the story. Other people's private thoughts are only ever guesses, from what those people say and do. The AI keeps later twists out of the instructions for earlier scenes.

That doesn't make the narration neutral. Your character can judge, guess and be completely wrong.

Changing Your Mind

You can change anything at any point. Just say so. The AI updates the plan, saves the old version, and redoes only the steps the change affects.

About The Reviewers

Some AI tools can start a separate helper with a fresh view of the work. When yours can, the machine uses one for each check, so the reviewer isn't grading its own homework. When it can't, the AI does the checks itself and says so in your handoff note.
