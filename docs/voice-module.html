Voice Module (Optional)

Want drafts that sound more like you from the start? The Voice Module studies writing you've already done.

You don't need this to write your first story. The machine works without it. Try a story first, then come back if you'd like the drafts closer to your voice.

What It Does

Stylometry means measuring writing style: sentence lengths, how much dialogue you use, your punctuation habits, the words you repeat. It can't judge whether writing is good, but it can show how your writing differs from an AI draft, in a way you can turn into instructions.

Where To Get It

The Voice Module is a separate free kit called Stylo Starter:

github.com/bytesizedbooksmith/stylo-starter

It has its own beginner guide and works the same way as this machine: open its folder in your AI tool and follow its start page.

Using It With The Machine

  1. Run Stylo Starter on a few pieces of your own finished writing.
  2. Come back to the Short Story Machine and tell the Guide: "Add my Stylo Starter voice notes to my style sheet," and point it to the results.
  3. The Guide adds the parts that apply to your style sheet, and every story after that uses them.

The Other Way Your Voice Gets In

Even without the Voice Module, the machine learns as you go. When you read the opening sample, any note that applies to all your writing ("I always use contractions") can be saved to your style sheet for next time.
