# Step 0 — Setup

**Goal:** a short style sheet for one pen name, so every story starts from the author's basic preferences. Aim for five minutes, not an hour.

**Creates:** `pen-names/<pen-name>/style-sheet.md` from [../templates/style-sheet.md](../templates/style-sheet.md).

## Do This

1. Welcome them briefly:
   - What the machine does: you plan the story together, the AI drafts and checks it, and they get a first draft to edit. They approve the plan and read the opening along the way.
   - The controls: `start` to begin or pick up where they left off, `help` to see where they are and what's next, `pause` to save and stop. Otherwise they can just talk normally.
   - The cost: drafting and checking uses a fair amount of their AI plan or credits. A short story takes about a dozen AI runs; long stories and lots of rewriting take much more.
2. Ask, three at a time at most:
   - Pen name (or "none yet" — use their first name for the folder).
   - Genre or genres they write.
   - Point of view and tense they prefer. Offer: first person past, third person past (one character), third person past (two characters), or "depends on the story."
3. Then ask:
   - Spelling: US, UK, or another (Canadian, Australian…).
   - Content limits: heat level, violence, language, anything they never want on the page.
   - Anything they already know they love or hate in AI writing. Give two or three examples if they're unsure (for example "too many em dashes," "everyone 'lets out a breath they didn't know they were holding'").
4. Fill in the style sheet. Leave anything they didn't answer as the template's default; don't invent preferences.
5. Mention the optional Voice Module in one sentence: it can study their past writing to tune the style sheet, and they can find it on the Voice Module page in the docs later. Don't run it now.

**Done when:** the style sheet exists. Update nothing else; there's no story yet. Go straight to Step 1.
