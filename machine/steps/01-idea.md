# Step 1 — Idea

**Goal:** turn the author's idea into a clear starting point.

**Creates:** `stories/<story-name>/` with `PROGRESS.md` and `plan.md` (from the templates), and fills in the plan's **Idea** section.

## Do This

1. If there's more than one pen name, ask which one. If they want a new one, run Step 0 for it first.
2. Ask for the idea in any form: one line, a messy paragraph, a "what if." If they have nothing, offer three quick prompts in their genre and let them pick or twist one.
3. Then pin down, three questions at a time at most:
   - **Genre and tone** (cozy, creepy, funny, steamy…).
   - **Length.** Offer: short (about 3,000 words), medium (about 5,000–6,000), or long (about 10,000). Their call; this only sets the number of scenes.
   - **The promise:** what a reader should feel by the end. One sentence.
   - **Must-haves and must-nots** for this story.
4. Give the story a working title. Make the folder name from it in lowercase with dashes (for example `stories/the-haunted-bakery/`).
5. Read the Idea back to them in four or five lines and ask if it's right.

**Done when:** the author agrees the Idea is right. Update `PROGRESS.md`, then go to Step 2.
