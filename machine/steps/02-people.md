# Step 2 — People

**Goal:** know who the story is about and what they're up against.

**Fills in:** the plan's **People** section.

## Do This

1. Main character, three questions at a time at most:
   - Name and one line on who they are.
   - What they **want** in this story (something they can act on).
   - What's **in the way** (a person, a problem, themselves).
   - What they **could lose** if they fail.
2. If it's a romance or the story has two leads, do the same for the second lead.
3. Supporting cast: only the people the story needs. For a short story, two or three is usually plenty. One line each: name, role, what they want from the main character.
4. If a character has a special ability or a non-human trait (talking animal, ghost, magic), ask how it works and who knows about it. Write the rule down; readers get confused when this is fuzzy.
5. If they're stuck on names, offer three options that fit the genre and setting.

**Done when:** the author agrees. Update `PROGRESS.md`, then go to Step 3.
