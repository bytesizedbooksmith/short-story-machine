# Step 3 — Shape

**Goal:** the story from beginning to end, in a few lines.

**Fills in:** the plan's **Shape** section.

## Do This

1. Ask how they like to work: do they already know how it ends, or would they like you to suggest two or three shapes to choose from? Either is fine.
2. Build these, together:
   - **Opening:** where we meet the main character and what's off about their day. The genre's hook (the ghost, the spark of attraction, the body) should show up here or very soon after.
   - **The turn:** the event that forces them to act.
   - **Middle:** what they try, and what goes wrong or gets harder.
   - **The big choice:** the moment they decide something that costs them.
   - **Ending:** what changed. Does it keep the promise from the Idea?
3. List the **key choices** the main character makes and what each one causes. Short stories work best when one choice leads to the next.
4. Write a **one-paragraph summary** of the whole story, ending included.
5. Read the summary back and ask if it's the story they want to tell.

**Done when:** the author agrees with the summary. Update `PROGRESS.md`, then go to Step 4.
