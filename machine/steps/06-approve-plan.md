# Step 6 — ✋ Approve The Plan

**Goal:** the author signs off. Nothing is drafted before this.

## Do This

1. Show the author, briefly:
   - The one-paragraph summary.
   - The scene list (number and one line each).
   - What the challenger found and what you fixed, in two or three lines.
   - Any questions from Step 5, each with your recommendation.
2. Ask them to answer the questions, then approve, or tell you what to change.
3. If they change something, update the plan and show only what changed. You don't need to rerun the challenger for small changes. Do rerun it if the ending, the main character's goal, or the order of scenes changed.
4. When they approve, set the plan's status to `approved (<date>)` and write in `PROGRESS.md`: "Plan approved", the date, and their exact words.

**Done when:** the author has clearly approved. "Looks good," "yes," and "go" count. Silence doesn't. Then go to Step 7.
