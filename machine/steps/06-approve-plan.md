# Step 6 — ✋ Approve The Plan

**Goal:** the author signs off. Nothing is drafted before this.

## Do This

1. Show the author, briefly:
   - The one-paragraph summary.
   - The scene list (number and one line each).
   - What the challenger found and what you fixed, in two or three lines.
   - Any questions from Step 5, each with your recommendation.
2. Ask them to answer the questions, then approve, or tell you what to change.
3. If they change something, update the plan and show only what changed. You don't need to rerun the challenger for small changes. Do rerun it if the ending, the main character's goal, or the order of scenes changed.
4. When they approve, set the plan's status to `approved (<date>)`, set the status in `PROGRESS.md` to `plan approved`, and write there: "Plan approved", the date, and their exact words.
5. **Offer the voice sample.** Ask one optional question: "Do you have a page of your own finished prose you'd like this draft to sound like? It's fine to skip this." It should be writing they wrote or edited themselves, not another author's. If they share one, save up to about a page of it as `writing/voice-sample.md`. If they skip it, carry on; don't ask again. This is separate from the Voice Module, which measures writing; the sample is simply read as an example.

**Done when:** the author has clearly approved and answered or skipped the voice sample. "Looks good," "yes," and "go" count. Silence doesn't. Then go to Step 7.
