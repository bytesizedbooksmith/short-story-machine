# Step 7 — Writing Plan

**Goal:** turn the approved plan into instructions the Drafter can follow, and make sure those instructions don't contradict each other.

**Creates:** `writing/brief.md` (from [../templates/writing-brief.md](../templates/writing-brief.md)) and one packet per scene in `writing/packets/` (from [../templates/scene-packet.md](../templates/scene-packet.md)).

## Do This

1. Write the **brief**: voice, point of view, tense, tone, the reader promise, content limits and the style rules that apply, all drawn from the style sheet and the plan. No new creative decisions.
2. Write one **packet per scene**. Each packet holds only what the Drafter needs for that scene:
   - The scene card.
   - Who's in it and the facts about them that matter here.
   - What the point-of-view character knows and doesn't know.
   - A short summary of what has happened so far (blank for Scene 1).
   - **Never** the ending or later twists, unless this scene has to set them up. Then say what to set up, not what it pays off.
   - Only the story rules this scene actually uses, marked **background: show through action, don't explain**.
   - For Scene 1, the chosen **Opening hook** first, and a pointer to [../guides/hooks.md](../guides/hooks.md).
3. **Instruction Conflict Check.** Read the brief, every packet, the style sheet and [../roles/drafter.md](../roles/drafter.md) side by side. Look for:
   - Two rules that can't both be followed ("keep it light" and "graphic violence").
   - Facts that differ between files (a name, an age, a timeline).
   - A packet that leaks something the character can't know yet.
   - A style rule the scene card would break.
4. Fix each conflict in the brief or packet. If the only fix changes the approved plan, ask the author one clear question with your recommendation.
5. Note the result in `reviews/conflict-check.md`: what you found and what you changed, or "No conflicts found."

**Done when:** the brief and packets exist and the conflict check is clean. Update `PROGRESS.md`, then go to Step 8.
