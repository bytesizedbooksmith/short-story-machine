# Step 10 — Three Audits

**Goal:** three separate reviewers look at the whole draft, each for a different kind of problem.

**Creates:** `reviews/audit-reader.md`, `reviews/audit-continuity.md`, `reviews/audit-style.md`.

## Do This

1. Put the scenes together, in order, into `writing/full-draft.md`. This is the version the auditors review.
2. Run all three roles on that same file. They don't see each other's reports.
   - **Reader** ([../roles/reader.md](../roles/reader.md)): gets the full draft and the Idea section only. It reads like a reader, without the plan.
   - **Continuity** ([../roles/continuity.md](../roles/continuity.md)): gets the full draft and the plan.
   - **Style** ([../roles/style.md](../roles/style.md)): gets the full draft, the brief and the style sheet.
3. If your tool can run separate agents, run these three as separate agents. Otherwise do them one at a time yourself and label each `self-review`.

**Done when:** all three reports are saved. Update `PROGRESS.md`, then go to Step 11.
