# Step 11 — Revise

**Goal:** fix what the audits found, without wandering away from the approved plan.

**Updates:** the scene files and `writing/full-draft.md`; adds a **Decisions** section to each audit report.

## Do This

1. Read all three audit reports together. Where they disagree, the approved plan and the style sheet decide. Where those are silent, choose what serves the reader promise.
2. For every finding, write a decision in its report: **fixed**, **rejected** (one-line reason), or **ask the author**.
3. Fix blockers first, then consequential problems. Optional preferences only if they're quick and clearly better.
4. Save each changed scene as a new version (the old one goes to `archive/`), then rebuild `full-draft.md`.
5. Recheck: run the role whose finding you fixed on the changed passages. A big change (a rewritten scene) gets a Continuity recheck too.
6. Two repair rounds at most. If a blocker survives, it becomes a question for the author at handoff, with your recommendation.

**Done when:** no blockers remain, or the remaining ones are written up as questions. Update `PROGRESS.md`, then go to Step 12.
