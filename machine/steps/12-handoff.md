# Step 12 — ✋ Final Check And Handoff

**Goal:** one last independent check, then hand the author a clean draft and a short note. After this, the story is theirs.

**Creates:** `reviews/final-challenge.md`, `FINALS/<Story Title> — v1.md` and `FINALS/handoff-note.md`.

## Do This

1. Run the **Challenger** with the **Final** lens. Give it `writing/full-draft.md`, the plan, the style sheet and the three audit reports with their decisions.
2. Fix supported findings (two rounds at most). Rebuild `full-draft.md` if anything changed.
3. Copy the final text into `FINALS/<Story Title> — v1.md`: the title, the byline (pen name), then the scenes with a blank line and `* * *` between them. No notes, labels or comments inside the story.
4. Write `FINALS/handoff-note.md` from [../templates/handoff-note.md](../templates/handoff-note.md): word count, what was checked (and whether by separate agents or self-review), what you fixed, anything left open, and two or three things you'd look at first when editing.
5. Set the status in `PROGRESS.md` to `handed off`.
6. Tell the author, briefly:
   - Where the story and the note are.
   - Any open questions, with your recommendation.
   - That the draft is theirs now: they can edit it themselves, or ask you for help editing in this chat.
   - That they can type `start` any time to begin a new story.

**Done when:** the files are in `FINALS/` and the author has been told.

## If The Author Asks For Edits After Handoff

Help them. Make each requested change, save a new version (`v2`, `v3`) in `FINALS/`, and move the older one to `archive/`. If a change affects later scenes (a name, a fact, an event), tell them where else it shows up.
